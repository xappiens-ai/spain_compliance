# Privacy Policy

Spain Compliance is a Frappe / ERPNext app. It runs inside the site where you install it, including sites hosted on Frappe Cloud.

- The app does not operate a service of its own and does not transmit your accounting data, invoices, customers or company records to Xappiens or to any other third party.
- All data stays in the database of the site where the app is installed, under the control of that site's administrator.
- The app does not include analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.

Support: [xappiens.com/contacto](https://www.xappiens.com/contacto).
